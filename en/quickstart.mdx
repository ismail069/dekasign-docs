---
title: "Get started with DekaSign"
description: "From registration and onboarding to a working first request."
---

<Steps>
<Step title="Create an account or accept an invitation">Create an organization through [Register](https://dekasign.com/register), verify email/Google and complete onboarding. Invitees open their invitation and complete account setup, phone number and email verification. One account email can belong to only one organization.</Step>
<Step title="Check Trial and organization">A 30-day Trial starts automatically when organization setup completes. Check the Trial banner, end date, Credits, seats and storage. Trial does not charge automatically. The Owner checks the organization name and timezone in Settings.</Step>
<Step title="Prepare people and access">Use standard categories or add them in Create Design → Categories. Invite another approver; self-approval is prohibited. Assign roles and category/Request Form permissions. A Direct Manager is required only when used by the workflow.</Step>
<Step title="Prepare a form and workflow">Create or use a Form Builder form, configure Actors/routes in Workflow Engines, Validate, test in Sandbox and Publish. Operation Task requires Operation staff assigned to its category.</Step>
<Step title="Publish a Request Form Template">Choose a category and a published workflow in Request Form Templates. The form follows the workflow. Add optional/mandatory default documents if needed, then save and publish the template.</Step>
<Step title="Submit and check the outcome">Requesters use Operations → My Requests. Approvers use My Works → My Approvals. Operation staff use My Works → My Tasks or Operations → Team Queue. Follow statuses and history through completion.</Step>
</Steps>

New Owners can follow the [complete setup checklist](/en/guides/owner-setup). See [roles and Access Profiles](/en/guides/access-profiles), [Request Form Templates](/en/guides/request-form-templates) and [Operation Task](/en/guides/operation-tasks).

<Note>Publish prepares a reusable version. Submit creates a request. Approved describes the approval decision; Operation Task and signing have separate completion statuses.</Note>
