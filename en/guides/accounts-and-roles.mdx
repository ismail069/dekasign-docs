## Register and sign in
Register with email or Google on the main site, verify and complete organization onboarding. A Google-created account may not have a local password. Google does not bypass DekaSign OTP/TOTP or permissions. Use the correct organization subdomain.

A **30-day Trial starts automatically** after organization onboarding. Check the Trial banner, end date, Credits, seats and storage on the dashboard or Plans & billing. Paid plans require verified payment; a redirect alone does not prove payment.

## Invitations and roles
The Owner invites an email not registered in any organization, selects roles, a job title and an Access Profile or manual permissions. The invitee enters their name, phone number and password, verifies email and joins through the invitation link. Phone numbers are contact information.

[Roles and Access Profiles](/en/guides/access-profiles) define authority. Owner/Admin manage Create Design, Approvers make decisions, Creators submit requests and Operation staff perform follow-up work. Roles can be combined. Manager relationships and Internal/External tags do not automatically grant permissions.

## Navigation
| Group | Contents |
|---|---|
| Create Design | Form Builder, Categories, Workflow Engines, Request Form Templates, Document Sign template; Owner/Admin only. |
| Operations | My Requests, Team Queue, Document Sign, Secure Document Sharing and Verify document. |
| My Works | My Tasks, My Approvals, My Delegation, My Signature and My Documents according to role. |
| Management | Organization Hierarchy, Access Profiles, Reports, channels/templates, billing, integrations, audits and Support as authorized. |
| Settings | Profile, account security and permitted organization settings. |

Email OTP protects sign-in; enable TOTP Authenticator in **Settings → Account security** if desired. Store recovery codes safely. Never share accounts, OTPs or invitation links.

New Owners can follow [Owner getting started](/en/guides/owner-setup). Self-approval is prohibited; prepare another approver before testing an Owner's own request.
