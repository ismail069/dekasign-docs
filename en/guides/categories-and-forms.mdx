## Manage Categories
Owners/Admins open **Create Design → Categories**. Choose **Add category**, enter its name and description, and save. Use **Edit** to update it. Categories cannot be deleted; archive is available only when no Request Form is associated.

Categories organize Request Forms, permissions, and work in Team Queue. Renaming a category updates its displayed queue category name. Creating one does not grant every member submission or approval rights.

## Create a Request Form
<Steps>
<Step title="Design in Form Builder">
Create the fields, validation, tables, and data sources you need.
</Step>
<Step title="Publish the workflow">
Connect the form to an Approval Workflow Engine. Configure its category, approvers, and routing, then Validate, test in Sandbox, and Publish.
</Step>
<Step title="Create the Request Form Template">
Open **Create Design → Request Form Templates**, choose an active category and published workflow. Its form follows the workflow. Add default documents if required, then save and publish.
</Step>
<Step title="Assign access">
The Owner selects requesters and approvers through Access Profiles or manual grants. Users see only forms they are authorized to use.
</Step>
</Steps>

Publishing a workflow does not replace creating its Request Form Template. Standard templates provide a starting point; check assignment and permissions before team use.

## Versions and submissions
Each submission pins its form, workflow, and document versions. Template edits do not change a process already running. **My Requests** handles submissions and tracking; category management belongs in **Create Design**.

If a form is missing, check publication, active category, category grants, and template access. See [Request Form Templates](/en/guides/request-form-templates).
