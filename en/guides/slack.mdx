---
title: "Receive Slack notifications"
description: "Connect a Slack workspace and confirm recipient accounts."
---

## Owner connects the workspace

Open **Management → Integrations → Slack → Connect Slack**. Select a Slack workspace and approve the requested permissions. Installation may require Slack administrator approval. Once connected, Slack appears in **Notification channels**. Manage the connection in **Integrations**.

## Each member confirms their account

1. Open **Settings → Personal integrations → Slack**.
2. Check the account matched by email. If your Slack email differs, use the email lookup option.
3. Request a confirmation code, open the Slack DM, and enter the code in DekaSign.

Eligible work notifications go to confirmed recipients by DM. Open the message link to review or act in DekaSign. Sign-in codes follow the account's security method. If no DM arrives, check the workspace, matching account, and confirmation status.
