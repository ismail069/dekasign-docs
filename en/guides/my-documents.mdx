---
title: "Manage My Documents"
description: "Find, upload, and copy documents you can access."
---

Open **My Works → My Documents** to find documents you are authorized to use.

- **Upload document** prepares a PDF source for later use. For a Drive document, select **Choose from Google Drive** after [connecting your account](/en/guides/google-drive). Complete the document details, review, and save.
- **Copy to Google Drive** is available for downloadable documents when a destination is connected. Choose a personal or organization folder if offered. The copy follows Google's sharing permissions.
- To request a signature, prepare and send a package in [Document Sign](/en/guides/document-sign). Uploading prepares the file without sending an invitation.
- To exchange documents, use [Secure Document Sharing](/en/guides/secure-sharing). To group personal work references, use [My Projects](/en/guides/my-projects).
