## Personal Drive

1. Open **Settings → Personal integrations → Google Drive**.
2. Select **Connect Google Drive**, choose your account, and grant the requested permissions. Google sign-in and Drive authorization are separate steps.
3. Choose or create an export folder on the Drive card. In [My Documents](/en/guides/my-documents), choose a Drive file to import or copy a document you can download.

Supported Google documents are converted to PDF on import. To change accounts, reconnect. Disconnect from **Personal integrations** when you no longer need access. Existing copies stay in Drive.

## Organization Shared Drive

The Owner configures **Management → Integrations → Google Drive · Organization**. Your Google administrator prepares the Shared Drive and destination folder and gives the organization service account write access. Enter the drive and folder IDs, then choose **Verify & enable**. Eligible internal members can then choose the organization folder when copying a document. Import uses the personal Drive connection.

Check folder permissions before copying. Access in DekaSign and access to the Drive copy are managed separately.
