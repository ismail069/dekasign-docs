---
title: "Build a workflow with the wizard"
description: "Set up a form, approval steps, and follow-up work in one place."
---

Open **Create Design → Workflow Engines → Create with wizard** to build a sequence of approvals. A starting example is a purchase request reviewed by a manager and fulfilled by Procurement.

1. **Form and category.** Choose an existing form or a solution preset. A preset creates a separate form you can edit.
2. **Approval steps.** Add reviewers in order. Choose named members or the requester's direct manager. Set revisions, deadlines, and reminders as needed.
3. **Tasks and notifications.** Choose follow-up work, eligible staff, SLA, and notification channels.
4. **Review and publish.** Save a draft if the process needs more work. Check permissions and publish to make the Request Form available to eligible requesters.

Test with separate requester and approver accounts. Use the [Canvas](/en/guides/approval-workflows) for advanced branches or parallel approval. If you edit the business rules in Canvas, continue editing there. Running requests keep the version used at submission.
