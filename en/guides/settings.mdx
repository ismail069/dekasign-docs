## Profile and availability
Open **Settings → Profile** to update personal information. Staff can set **Not available until** during leave or absence. New Operation Task assignments consider availability; claimed tasks retain their current holder.

## Account security
Email OTP is the default after account authentication. Codes expire after five minutes, with resend cooldowns and attempt limits. Google accounts use Google identity followed by required DekaSign verification; this does not imply they have a local password.

For an authenticator, open **Account security → Set up authenticator**, complete the requested verification, scan the QR, and keep recovery codes privately. Enrollment takes effect after code confirmation. Subsequent sign-in uses TOTP or a recovery code; Email OTP is not a fallback while TOTP is active.

Choose **Switch to Email OTP** to change methods, then verify your password if the account has one and supply TOTP/recovery verification. Security changes may revoke old sessions. Never send OTPs, passwords, or recovery codes to support.

## Organization
Owners use **Organization** to manage name, email domain, logo, favicon, and theme. The customer's primary logo appears in organization contexts; DekaSign is the default without an uploaded logo.

The default timezone is **Asia/Jakarta (GMT+7)**. Select a global timezone appropriate to the organization. Displayed dates/times and filters follow it; stored times remain consistent. Check timezone before setting schedules or deadlines.

## User management
Invite a new email not registered to any DekaSign organization. Choose role, job title, Access Profile or manual permissions, categories, and Direct Manager when available. Invitees set a password, enter their phone number, and verify email through the invitation flow.

Use a member row's action menu for access, Internal/External tags, MFA recovery, status, and document review. Bulk invitation accepts CSV/XLSX using downloadable templates. Review the import preview before sending.

The Owner cannot be deleted or have their role revoked through ordinary member actions. Use **Transfer Ownership** to hand the organization to an eligible member and follow its security confirmation. Manage managers and Access Profiles through their Management pages.

## Recovery and handover
**Recover MFA** helps members who lost their authenticator and recovery codes. The Owner completes fresh security verification, reviews the target, and enters a reason. Recovery returns the member to Email OTP without granting document access to the Owner.

**Review documents** opens a popup to inspect inventory before handover. Reviewing alone transfers nothing. Choose the receiving account, review conflicts, then confirm an allowed action. Signer identities, decisions, and historical audit remain unchanged.

## Integrations
Integration and API settings follow Owner role and available workspace permissions. Store keys in a server-side secret manager, never frontend code or URLs.
