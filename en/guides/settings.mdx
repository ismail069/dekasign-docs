## Personal settings
Open the profile menu in the top bar, then **Settings**. Check your name and account identity. To change your password, complete the form's verification and password requirements. Do not request password changes through comments or support messages.

## Sign-in verification
In the latest local implementation, **Settings → Account security** shows your sign-in method. Email OTP is enabled by default for internal accounts. After a correct password, enter the code sent to your registered email before accessing the workspace. Codes expire after five minutes; resend cooldown and attempt limits apply.

To use an authenticator, sign in again if your session is older than five minutes, select **Set up authenticator**, verify your password if your account has one, and scan the QR. Save ten recovery codes privately and confirm an authenticator code. Email OTP remains active until confirmation succeeds. Enrollment revokes old sessions; subsequent sign-in uses TOTP or one recovery code. Email OTP is unavailable as a fallback while TOTP is active.

To return to Email OTP, select **Switch to Email OTP**, provide your password if required, and enter a fresh authenticator code or an unused recovery code. Switching revokes the authenticator, recovery codes and all sessions; sign in again with Email OTP. Document ownership transfer remains unavailable in this increment.

## Owner-assisted MFA recovery
In the latest local implementation, the Owner opens **Settings → User management → Recover MFA** for a member who lost their phone and recovery codes. The Owner's sign-in must have completed MFA within the last five minutes. Select **Review recovery**, check the member's email, enter a reason, type that email and verify the Owner password if the account has one.

Confirmation switches the member's authenticator to Email OTP and revokes the old keys, recovery codes and sessions. The member signs in again with their own credentials and a code at their registered email. Recovery preserves email/password, member status, permissions and document ownership; it grants the Owner no document access. The action is audited atomically. If the result is uncertain, **Retry same recovery** repeats the exact command.

Recovery is limited to the Owner's workspace. Admins, guest identities and legacy accounts still registered in multiple workspaces cannot use this flow. Use personal recovery under Account security for your own account. Migration and production rollout still require release verification.

<Note>This flow has been tested on a local server and desktop/mobile browsers. Retained database migration, operator authentication SMTP readiness and production rollout remain pending. Sign-in OTP delivery is separate from organization notification channels.</Note>

## Organization settings
The Owner opens **Organization** in Settings. Enter the organization name and authorized email domain, upload a logo, and choose theme colors. Preview branding before saving. The email domain classifies internal/external recipients; an internal label is not proof of legal authority.

Organization branding appears in the relevant workspace context, including tenant login when configured. Do not enter another organization's information if the subdomain is wrong or the organization cannot be found.

## Members and permissions
Use **User management / Members** according to your role. Invite the correct email, choose the minimum necessary role, and grant category/form permissions. Review active status and validity periods. Do not grant Owner merely to let someone approve; grant the appropriate category authority.

## Workspace API keys
The Owner opens **Settings → API Access**. Create an integration app, choose its mapped requester, allowed categories/workflows/templates, minimum scopes, and key expiry. Copy the key once into your server-side secret manager. Rotate or revoke exposed keys. Never put keys in forms, frontend code, URLs, or source repositories.

Management APIs remain sandbox-only and use workspace data within allowlists. Sandbox does not automatically isolate data. Do not use the API for customer production transactions before the required activation and commercial scope are available.

## Operational checklist
Check the organization, membership, category grants, tested email channel, published form/workflow, and substitute approvers. See [notification channels](/en/guides/notification-channels).
