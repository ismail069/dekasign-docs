## Application features
Guides cover Form Builder, Categories, Request Form Templates, Workflow Canvas, serial/parallel ALL/ANY approval, external Actors, revisions, delegation, Document Sign, Secure Document Sharing, My Signature, Organization Hierarchy, Access Profiles, Operation Task, My Projects, Contract Lifecycle, Workflow Wizard, Owner Reports, and the Team Queue report.

Actions depend on roles, categories, document permissions, subscription status, and workflow version. A sample design does not grant access to another user's data. Check readiness with the [Owner setup guide](/en/guides/owner-setup).

## APIs and providers
Use Sandbox APIs for testing. Production APIs require an active paid plan, Production integration app/key, and allowed scopes. Catalog, submission, signing, attachment, and revision support follow available configuration; do not assume every Sandbox operation is available in Production. Management REST APIs remain sandbox-only for design-management operations not supported in Production.

Custom channels require provider credentials and a successful test. Accepted does not mean delivered/read. Payments activate capacity only after provider verification; a browser redirect alone is not evidence of payment.

## Connections that require setup
[Google Drive](/en/guides/google-drive), [organization Calendar](/en/guides/google-calendar), [Slack](/en/guides/slack), [HRIS/ERP import](/en/guides/employee-import), and [n8n](/en/integrations/n8n) require setup. Google sign-in, Drive, and Calendar use separate permissions. Check available enterprise SSO or other connectors with Support.

DekaSign integrity evidence does not claim a certified electronic seal, third-party signer certificate, trusted timestamp, or external KYC verification. Choose a method appropriate to your transaction.

## Help
If a screen or action differs, note its process reference, timestamp, and error message. Contact support@dekasign.com without sharing OTPs, passwords, API keys, or private links.
